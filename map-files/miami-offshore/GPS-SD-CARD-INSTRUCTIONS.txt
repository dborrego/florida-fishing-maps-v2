  1. BEFORE YOU START -- WHAT YOU NEED
================================================================================

Gather these items before starting:

  [ ] MicroSD card (2GB to 32GB recommended -- see Section 9)
  [ ] MicroSD to SD adapter (if your computer uses full-size SD slot)
  [ ] MicroSD card reader (USB or built-in on your computer)
  [ ] Your downloaded GPS files from FloridaFishingMaps.com
  [ ] Your chartplotter's user manual (for reference)

IMPORTANT: Do NOT use the SD card that came with your chartplotter if it
contains map data (Navionics, C-MAP, LakeMaster, etc.). Use a SEPARATE card
for importing waypoints. You do not want to accidentally format a card with
your expensive map chip on it.

================================================================================
  2. FORMATTING YOUR SD CARD (CRITICAL STEP)
================================================================================

Most marine electronics ONLY read FAT32-formatted cards. This is the single
most common reason for "card not recognized" errors.

*** WARNING: FORMATTING ERASES ALL DATA ON THE CARD. ***
*** BACK UP ANY EXISTING DATA BEFORE FORMATTING.     ***

WINDOWS:
  1. Insert the SD card into your computer.
  2. Open File Explorer and right-click on the SD card drive.
  3. Select "Format."
  4. Set File System to "FAT32."
     - NOTE: Windows will not show FAT32 as an option for cards larger
       than 32GB. If your card is 64GB or larger, use the free tool
       "FAT32 Format" (guiformat.com) or "Rufus" to force FAT32.
  5. Set Allocation Unit Size to "Default."
  6. Check "Quick Format."
  7. Click "Start."

MAC:
  1. Insert the SD card into your computer.
  2. Open Disk Utility (Applications > Utilities > Disk Utility).
  3. Select the SD card from the left panel.
  4. Click "Erase."
  5. Set Format to "MS-DOS (FAT)" -- this is Mac's label for FAT32.
  6. Set Scheme to "Master Boot Record."
  7. Click "Erase."

IMPORTANT NOTES ON FORMATTING:
  - exFAT will NOT work on most marine electronics. Use FAT32.
  - NTFS will NOT work on any marine electronics. Use FAT32.
  - APFS (Mac) will NOT work. Use MS-DOS (FAT).
  - If your card was previously used in a camera or phone, it may be in a
    format that your chartplotter cannot read. Always reformat before use.

  3. GARMIN SD CARD LOADING
================================================================================

Compatible Units: GPSMAP 7x2/9x2/10x2/12x2/16x2, ECHOMAP UHD/UHD2,
ECHOMAP Plus, GPSMAP 8400/8600 series, GPSMAP 1x4x series.

STEP-BY-STEP:

  1. Format your SD card as FAT32 (see Section 2).

  2. Insert the formatted card into your computer.

  3. Open the SD card in File Explorer (Windows) or Finder (Mac).

  4. Create a folder structure on the card:
       SD Card Root/
         Garmin/
           GPX/

  5. Copy your .GPX file(s) into the "GPX" folder.
     The folder path should read: [SD Card]/Garmin/GPX/yourfile.gpx

  6. Safely eject the SD card from your computer.
     - Windows: Right-click the drive > "Eject"
     - Mac: Drag the card icon to the trash, or click the eject icon

  7. Insert the SD card into your Garmin chartplotter.
     - Most Garmin units have two card slots on the front or side.
     - Either slot works for data import.

  8. Power on the Garmin unit.

  9. Navigate to: Info > User Data > Data Transfer > Import from Card.
     - On newer GPSMAP units: NavInfo > Manage Data > Data Transfer.

  10. Select the SD card slot where you inserted the card.

  11. Select "Import Waypoints" or "Import All" depending on your preference.

  12. Select the .GPX file from the list.

  13. Confirm the import. Wait for the progress bar to complete.

  14. Your spots are now saved to internal memory. Navigate to your chart
      view and you should see the waypoint markers.

GARMIN-SPECIFIC NOTES:
  - After import, waypoints are stored in internal memory. The SD card can
    be removed.
  - To verify: go to Info > User Data > Waypoints. You should see your
    imported spots listed.
  - Use "Edit Waypoint" to change colors or icons for easier identification.
  - The folder MUST be named "Garmin" with a capital G. Lowercase will not
    be recognized on some models.

  4. LOWRANCE SD CARD LOADING
================================================================================

Compatible Units: HDS Live, HDS Carbon, HDS Gen3/Gen2, Elite Ti2,
Elite FS, Hook Reveal, Hook2.

STEP-BY-STEP:

  1. Format your SD card as FAT32 (see Section 2).

  2. Insert the formatted card into your computer.

  3. Copy the .GPX file(s) directly to the ROOT of the SD card.
     No special folder structure is needed for Lowrance.
     The path should be: [SD Card]/yourfile.gpx

  4. Safely eject the SD card.

  5. Insert the card into your Lowrance unit.
     - HDS Live/Carbon: card slot is on the front behind the rubber door.
     - Hook/Elite: card slot location varies; check your manual.

  6. Power on the unit.

  7. For HDS Live / HDS Carbon:
       a. Press the "Files" key on the home screen.
       b. Navigate to your SD card.
       c. Select the .GPX file.
       d. Choose "Import" when prompted.

  8. For Elite Ti2 / Hook Reveal:
       a. Navigate to: Settings > Navigation > Waypoints.
       b. Select "Import from Card."
       c. Choose the .GPX file.

  9. For older HDS Gen2/Gen3:
       a. Navigate to: Files > My Card.
       b. Browse to the GPX file.
       c. Select "View" or "Import."

  10. Wait for import to complete. A confirmation message will appear.

LOWRANCE-SPECIFIC NOTES:
  - Lowrance saves waypoints as "User Points" in internal memory.
  - To verify: check Waypoints in the Navigation menu.
  - If your unit says "unsupported file format," make sure you are running
    the latest firmware. Update at lowrance.com.
  - Hook2 units: these have limited waypoint capacity (typically 3,000).
    If you have a large spot package, prioritize the areas you fish most.

  5. HUMMINBIRD SD CARD LOADING
================================================================================

Compatible Units: HELIX 5/7/8/9/10/12/15, SOLIX 10/12/15, APEX series.

STEP-BY-STEP:

  1. Format your SD card as FAT32 (see Section 2).

  2. Insert the formatted card into your computer.

  3. Copy the .GPX file(s) to the ROOT of the SD card.
     Path: [SD Card]/yourfile.gpx

  4. Safely eject the SD card.

  5. Insert the card into your Humminbird unit.

  6. Power on the unit.

  7. For HELIX 7 and newer (with GPX support):
       a. Press the MENU key twice to access the main menu.
       b. Navigate to: Nav > Waypoints Management > Import.
       c. Select "SD Card" as the source.
       d. Choose the .GPX file.
       e. Confirm the import.

  8. For SOLIX series:
       a. Tap the chart screen.
       b. Navigate to: Chart Menu > Waypoints > Import.
       c. Select the SD card and choose your file.

  9. For HELIX 5 and older units (NO native GPX support):
       a. You must first convert GPX to Humminbird's .HWR format.
       b. Download HumminbirdPC from humminbird.com (free).
       c. Open HumminbirdPC and import the GPX file.
       d. Export as .HWR format to the SD card.
       e. Insert the card and import using the Nav menu.

  10. Wait for import confirmation.

HUMMINBIRD-SPECIFIC NOTES:
  - HELIX 5 units DO NOT support GPX natively. You must convert to HWR.
  - SOLIX units support drag-and-drop when connected via Ethernet to a PC.
  - If you get a "file error," check that the GPX file name does not contain
    special characters. Rename to something simple like "spots.gpx."
  - Humminbird has a waypoint limit per unit. HELIX 7+ supports 5,000+.
    HELIX 5 supports around 2,500.

  6. SIMRAD SD CARD LOADING
================================================================================

Compatible Units: NSS evo3/evo3S, NSX 3007/3009/3012, GO5/GO7/GO9/GO12,
Cruise series.

STEP-BY-STEP:

  1. Format your SD card as FAT32 (see Section 2).

  2. Insert the formatted card into your computer.

  3. Copy the .GPX file(s) to the ROOT of the SD card.

  4. Safely eject the SD card.

  5. Insert the card into your Simrad unit.

  6. Power on the unit.

  7. For NSS evo3 / evo3S:
       a. Press the "Home" button.
       b. Navigate to: Settings > Waypoints > Import.
       c. Select the SD card source.
       d. Choose the .GPX file and confirm.

  8. For NSX series:
       a. Open the "Data" menu from the home screen.
       b. Select "Import from Card."
       c. Choose the GPX file.

  9. For GO series / Cruise:
       a. Navigate to: Files > SD Card.
       b. Select the GPX file and choose "Import."

  10. Wait for the import to finish.

SIMRAD-SPECIFIC NOTES:
  - Simrad and Lowrance are both Navico products. The import process is
    very similar between brands.
  - NSX runs a different OS than NSS. If instructions for one don't work,
    check the other set of steps above.
  - Simrad Cruise units have the most limited import options. If the unit
    does not recognize your file, update firmware first.

  7. RAYMARINE SD CARD LOADING
================================================================================

Compatible Units: Axiom, Axiom+, Axiom Pro, Axiom XL, Element, Element HV,
Element S, eS series, a series.

STEP-BY-STEP:

  1. Format your SD card as FAT32 (see Section 2).

  2. Insert the formatted card into your computer.

  3. Copy the .GPX file(s) to the ROOT of the SD card or into a folder
     named "Raymarine" on the card.

  4. Safely eject the SD card.

  5. Insert the card into your Raymarine unit.

  6. Power on the unit.

  7. For Axiom / Axiom+ / Axiom Pro (LightHouse 4):
       a. Tap the screen and swipe down to access the app drawer.
       b. Open "My Data."
       c. Tap "Import/Export."
       d. Select "Import from Card."
       e. Browse to the GPX file and select it.
       f. Choose "Import Waypoints" and confirm.

  8. For Element / Element HV (LightHouse Sport):
       a. Press the MENU button.
       b. Navigate to: My Data > Import.
       c. Select the SD card.
       d. Choose the GPX file and import.

  9. For older eS / a Series (LightHouse 2/3):
       a. Navigate to: Home > My Data > Waypoints > Import.
       b. Select the SD card source.
       c. Choose the file and confirm.

  10. Wait for import confirmation.

RAYMARINE-SPECIFIC NOTES:
  - Make sure you are running LightHouse 4.1+ on Axiom units and
    LightHouse Sport OS 4.0+ on Element units for best GPX compatibility.
  - Axiom Pro units also support USB import via a direct PC connection.
  - The older Dragonfly series has limited import capabilities. Contact us
    if you need help with a Dragonfly unit.

  8. BACKUP YOUR EXISTING WAYPOINTS
================================================================================

Before importing new spots, we STRONGLY recommend backing up your existing
waypoints. If something goes wrong during import, you don't want to lose
years of personal fishing data.

HOW TO BACKUP:

  GARMIN:
    Info > User Data > Data Transfer > Export to Card > All User Data

  LOWRANCE:
    Files > My Data > Export > All Waypoints > Save to Card

  HUMMINBIRD:
    Nav > Waypoints Management > Export All > SD Card

  SIMRAD:
    Settings > Waypoints > Export to Card

  RAYMARINE:
    My Data > Import/Export > Export to Card > All Waypoints

BACKUP TIPS:
  - Use a DIFFERENT SD card for your backup than the one you're using
    for import. Keep the backup card in a safe, dry place.
  - Label the backup card with the date and the unit model.
  - Back up regularly, not just before imports. After every good fishing
    trip where you mark new spots, back up your data.
  - Some captains keep a quarterly backup rotation. Smart practice.

================================================================================
  9. CARD SIZE AND BRAND RECOMMENDATIONS
================================================================================

RECOMMENDED CARDS:
  - SanDisk Ultra 16GB or 32GB microSD (most reliable across all brands)
  - Samsung EVO Select 16GB or 32GB microSD
  - Kingston Canvas Select 16GB or 32GB microSD

CARD SIZE GUIDELINES:
  - 2GB to 32GB: Works with all marine electronics. Best compatibility.
  - 64GB: May work but requires forced FAT32 formatting (see Section 2).
  - 128GB+: Not recommended. Many units will not recognize cards this large.
  - For waypoint files only, a 2GB card is more than sufficient. Save the
    big cards for sonar recording and map storage.

WHAT TO AVOID:
  - Cheap no-name cards from Amazon. They often report false capacity and
    can corrupt data.
  - Cards you've been using in a GoPro or dashcam for years. Heavily-used
    cards are more likely to fail.
  - Full-size SD cards without adapters (most modern units use microSD).

================================================================================
  10. COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID
================================================================================

  MISTAKE: Using exFAT or NTFS format.
  FIX: Always format as FAT32. See Section 2.

  MISTAKE: Putting files in the wrong folder (or wrong level) on Garmin cards.
  FIX: Garmin requires [SD Card]/Garmin/GPX/ folder structure. Other brands
  accept files in the root directory.

  MISTAKE: Not safely ejecting the card from the computer.
  FIX: Always use "Safely Remove Hardware" (Windows) or "Eject" (Mac).
  Pulling the card without ejecting can corrupt the file.

  MISTAKE: Importing on top of existing waypoints without backing up first.
  FIX: Always back up your waypoints before importing. See Section 8.

  MISTAKE: Using a card with map data (Navionics, C-MAP) for waypoint import.
  FIX: Use a separate, dedicated card for data transfer.

  MISTAKE: Trying to import on a unit with outdated firmware.
  FIX: Update your chartplotter firmware to the latest version before
  attempting any file import. Check the manufacturer's website.

  MISTAKE: Card is too large (64GB+) for the unit to recognize.
  FIX: Use a 16GB or 32GB card. If you must use 64GB+, force FAT32 format.
