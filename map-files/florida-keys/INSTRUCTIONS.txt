  1. GARMIN CHARTPLOTTERS (GPSMAP, ECHOMAP, ECHOMAP UHD)
================================================================================

Garmin units accept GPX files natively. This is the most straightforward
process of any brand.

METHOD A - Via SD Card (Recommended):
  1. Insert a FAT32-formatted microSD card into your computer using a card
     reader or adapter.
  2. Create a folder on the card named "Garmin" (if one does not already exist).
  3. Inside the "Garmin" folder, create a subfolder called "GPX".
  4. Copy your .GPX file(s) into the Garmin/GPX folder.
  5. Safely eject the SD card from your computer.
  6. Insert the card into your Garmin chartplotter.
  7. Power on the unit. Navigate to: Info > User Data > Data Transfer > Import.
  8. Select the SD card and choose your GPX file.
  9. Your waypoints will appear on the chart.

METHOD B - Via Garmin HomePort / ActiveCaptain:
  1. Install Garmin HomePort (free) or the ActiveCaptain app on your phone.
  2. Connect your chartplotter via USB or WiFi (ActiveCaptain).
  3. Import the GPX file into HomePort, then transfer to your device.
  4. On ActiveCaptain: use the "Quickdraw Community" or data sync feature.

GARMIN TIPS:
  - Most Garmin units have TWO card slots. Either slot works for data import.
  - After import, your spots are saved to internal memory. You can remove the
    SD card once the transfer is complete.
  - To organize spots, use the "Categories" feature on your Garmin to group
    fishing spots by type (wrecks, reefs, ledges, etc.).

  2. LOWRANCE / HDS UNITS (HDS Live, HDS Carbon, Elite Ti2, Hook Reveal)
================================================================================

Lowrance units accept GPX files. Older units may require conversion to USR
format using Insight Genesis.

METHOD A - Via SD Card:
  1. Format a microSD card as FAT32 on your computer.
  2. Copy your .GPX file(s) directly to the root of the SD card. No special
     folder structure is required for Lowrance.
  3. Safely eject the card and insert it into your Lowrance unit.
  4. Power on the unit. Navigate to: Files > My Card > (select your GPX file).
  5. Tap "Import" or "View" depending on your software version.
  6. For HDS Live/Carbon: Settings > Navigation > Waypoints > Import from Card.

METHOD B - Via Insight Genesis (Online):
  1. Go to insightgenesis.lowrance.com and create an account.
  2. Upload your GPX file to your Insight Genesis account.
  3. Sync to your unit via SD card or WiFi (HDS Live units only).

LOWRANCE TIPS:
  - Elite Ti2 and Hook Reveal units: use the "Files" button on the home screen.
  - If your unit does not recognize the GPX file, rename the file extension to
    .usr and try again -- some older firmware versions are picky.
  - Always update your firmware before importing. Lowrance frequently adds
    format support in updates.

  3. HUMMINBIRD (HELIX, SOLIX, MEGA Series)
================================================================================

Humminbird uses its own .HWR (Humminbird Waypoint) format, but newer units
also accept GPX files directly.

METHOD A - Via SD Card (HELIX 7 and newer, all SOLIX):
  1. Format a microSD card as FAT32.
  2. Copy the .GPX file(s) to the root of the SD card.
  3. Insert the card into your Humminbird unit.
  4. Navigate to: Nav > Waypoints > Import > SD Card.
  5. Select the GPX file and import.

METHOD B - Via HumminbirdPC (Older Units):
  1. Download HumminbirdPC software from humminbird.com.
  2. Use the software to convert your GPX file to HWR format.
  3. Save the .HWR file to the root of a FAT32 SD card.
  4. Insert into your unit and import via the Nav menu.

METHOD C - Via Humminbird FishSmart App:
  1. Open the FishSmart app on your phone.
  2. Import the GPX file from your email or downloads folder.
  3. Sync to your Humminbird unit via Bluetooth or WiFi (SOLIX/MEGA only).

HUMMINBIRD TIPS:
  - HELIX 5 units: must use HumminbirdPC to convert to HWR format.
  - SOLIX units: the import process is under Chart > Menu > Waypoints.
  - Maximum waypoint capacity varies by model. HELIX 7+ supports 5,000+
    waypoints. Check your manual if you're loading large spot packages.

  4. SIMRAD (NSS evo3/evo3S, NSX, GO Series)
================================================================================

Simrad units share the same operating system as Lowrance (Navico). The process
is nearly identical to Lowrance.

METHOD A - Via SD Card:
  1. Format a microSD card as FAT32.
  2. Copy the .GPX file(s) to the root of the SD card.
  3. Insert the card into your Simrad unit.
  4. Navigate to: Settings > Waypoints > Import > SD Card.
  5. Select your file and confirm import.
  6. On NSS evo3: Files > Import Waypoints from Card.

METHOD B - Via Simrad App:
  1. Open the Simrad Companion app on your phone.
  2. Connect to your Simrad unit via WiFi.
  3. Import waypoints through the app's data management feature.

SIMRAD TIPS:
  - NSX units run a different operating system than older NSS units. On NSX,
    the import function is under the "Data" menu.
  - Simrad GO series: use the "Files" key on the home screen.

================================================================================
  5. RAYMARINE (Axiom, Axiom+, Axiom Pro, Element, Element HV)
================================================================================

Raymarine units accept GPX files via SD card or WiFi through the Raymarine app.

METHOD A - Via SD Card:
  1. Format a microSD card as FAT32.
  2. Create a folder on the card called "Raymarine" (optional but recommended).
  3. Copy the .GPX file(s) into the folder or the root of the card.
  4. Insert the card into the Axiom or Element unit.
  5. Navigate to: Menu > My Data > Import/Export > Import from Card.
  6. Select the GPX file and confirm.

METHOD B - Via Raymarine App / WiFi:
  1. Connect your Axiom to your phone via WiFi.
  2. Open the Raymarine app and use the "Waypoints" sync feature.
  3. Import GPX data through the app interface.

RAYMARINE TIPS:
  - Element series: make sure you are running LightHouse Sport OS 4.0+.
  - Axiom Pro: supports drag-and-drop when connected to a PC via USB.
  - Dragonfly units (older): use the Raymarine Waypoint Import tool on a
    computer to convert and transfer.

  6. MOBILE APPS
================================================================================

GOOGLE EARTH (iOS / Android / Desktop):
  1. Open the .KML or .KMZ file from your email or downloads folder.
  2. Your phone should offer to open it in Google Earth automatically.
  3. If not, open Google Earth, tap the menu, select "Projects" > "Import KML."
  4. Your fishing spots will appear as pins on the map.
  5. Tap any pin to see spot details, coordinates, and depth info.

NAVIONICS BOATING APP (iOS / Android):
  1. Open the Navionics Boating app.
  2. Tap the menu icon > "My Data" > "Import GPX."
  3. Browse to your downloaded .GPX file and select it.
  4. Your waypoints will load onto the Navionics chart.
  5. Community features: you can overlay your spots with Navionics SonarChart.

iBOATING APP (iOS / Android):
  1. Open iBoating and navigate to the "Markers" or "Routes" section.
  2. Tap "Import" and select the GPX file from your device.
  3. Spots will appear as markers on your iBoating charts.

GENERAL MOBILE TIPS:
  - If your phone does not offer to open the file in the correct app, try
    using a file manager app to navigate to the download and tap to open.
  - Most fishing apps also accept manual coordinate entry. The CSV file
    included in your download has all coordinates listed for easy reference.

  7. COMPUTER USE
================================================================================

GOOGLE EARTH PRO (Free Desktop Software):
  1. Download and install Google Earth Pro from google.com/earth.
  2. Open Google Earth Pro.
  3. Go to File > Open.
  4. Change the file type filter to "KML/KMZ" or "GPS (GPX)."
  5. Select your file and click Open.
  6. Your spots will appear in the "Places" panel on the left side.
  7. Double-click any spot to fly to it on the map.
  8. Use the ruler tool to measure distances from your marina or inlet.

OPENCPN (Free Marine Charting Software):
  1. Download OpenCPN from opencpn.org.
  2. Install and add your preferred charts (NOAA is free).
  3. Go to the Route & Waypoint Manager (Ctrl+W).
  4. Click "Import GPX" and select your file.
  5. Spots will appear on your chart.

MICROSOFT EXCEL / GOOGLE SHEETS (CSV Files):
  1. Open the .CSV file in Excel or Google Sheets.
  2. Each row contains: Spot Name, Latitude, Longitude, Depth, Type, Notes.
  3. Use this for manual entry into any chartplotter, GPS, or app.
  4. Sort and filter by depth, type, or area to find exactly what you need.

  8. TROUBLESHOOTING TIPS
================================================================================

  - FILE NOT RECOGNIZED: Make sure your SD card is formatted as FAT32, not
    exFAT or NTFS. Most marine electronics only read FAT32.

  - WAYPOINTS NOT SHOWING: After import, you may need to enable the waypoint
    layer on your chart display. Check your overlay or layer settings.

  - TOO MANY WAYPOINTS: Some older units have waypoint limits (500-2,000).
    If you hit the limit, split the GPX file into smaller sections using a
    free tool like GPX Editor (gpx.studio).

  - SD CARD NOT READING: Try a different card. Some units are picky about
    card brands or sizes. Cards over 32GB may not format as FAT32 natively
    on Windows -- use a tool like Rufus or FAT32 Format to force it.

  - COORDINATES LOOK WRONG: Our files use WGS84 datum (the universal GPS
    standard). Make sure your chartplotter is set to WGS84, not NAD27 or
    another datum.

  - STILL STUCK? Email us at support@floridafishingmaps.com with your device
    model and a screenshot of the issue. We'll walk you through it.
